# Jan - Local AI Chat Client

## About Jan

Jan is a local AI chat client that runs models directly on your computer. It works as an open source alternative to cloud chat services, letting you talk to an assistant without sending your prompts or files to a remote server. Jan keeps the whole session on your own machine, so your notes, drafts, and questions stay where you put them. For people who want a private AI assistant, Jan offers a calm, readable workspace that feels like a normal desktop chat window but runs the model locally.

Jan is built for everyday use. You open the client, pick a model, and start a conversation. There is no account wall and no waiting on a remote queue. Because Jan runs models offline, it keeps working when your connection drops or when you would rather not use one at all. The project is open source, so anyone can read the code, check how it handles data, and suggest changes.

Many people search for a ChatGPT alternative because they want the same kind of back and forth without handing over every message. Jan answers that need by keeping the model and the conversation on your hardware. You can ask questions, draft text, summarize notes, or think through a problem, and the chat stays local. That makes Jan a practical choice for students, writers, developers, and anyone who cares about where their data lives.

Jan also fits people who simply prefer to run models offline. Once a model is on your disk, Jan does not need the internet to hold a conversation. You can work on a train, in a café with weak Wi-Fi, or on a machine that is deliberately kept off the network. The client stays responsive, the chat stays readable, and your private AI assistant stays yours.
